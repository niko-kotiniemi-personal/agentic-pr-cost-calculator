# Changelog

Every change that an adopter of the calculator would notice is listed here, newest first, in the form [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) describes. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html): the major number rises for a change that adopters must act on, the minor number for new behaviour, and the patch number for a fix. Design decision 11 of `add-pr-cost-calculator` defines what counts as each.

A release's entry lists any breaking change under `### Breaking`, and the release workflow refuses a release that lists one without raising the major number.
