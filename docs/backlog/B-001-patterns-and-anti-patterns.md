## Problem

The design is large and has no code yet. Without a written assessment, a pattern that the project follows by choice and an anti-pattern that it has drifted into look alike, and developers and agents that work here have no page that says, concern by concern, how the project builds and where it stands.

## What done looks like

A guide says how the project builds, concern by concern, such as architecture and integration, security, privacy, identity and credentials, supply chain, testing, and decisions and documentation: for each, the principles that the project follows, the patterns chosen, the anti-patterns to steer clear of, what holds each, and where the project stands, so that the project can follow its own progress as the tool grows. Developers and agents follow it below the specs and `CLAUDE.md`, which win where it differs from them (DR-231). Each anti-pattern that the assessment of 2026-11-12 found is either knowingly accepted, with the record that gives the reason, or a gap that names the backlog item that closes it. Its entry on decisions says that each record covers one topic and says how hard it is to reverse, and that replacing one starts an assessment of what rests on it (DR-230).
